About

AN OPEN PRACTICE

I work across writing, translation, creative production and project development. I’m interested in how an idea can find the right form, audience and route into everyday life.

My work moves between cultural initiatives and digital environments, between language and image, independent authorship and collaboration. I value clear thinking, strong concepts and practical ways to make creative work happen.
